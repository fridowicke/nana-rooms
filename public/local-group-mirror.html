Local Gr0up is a platform from Ukraine that emerged in 2021, dedicated to showcasing contemporary experimental visions and fostering connections across borders. Ranging from publications to film screenings and exhibitions, it evolved from Local Stickerbook, a sticker-based magazine that laid its foundation.

Local Gr0up's publications function as post-internet archives, using playful and accessible formats to document emerging Ukrainian and global subcultures. Together, they reflect a generation navigating identity and technology within an accelerated hypermediated present.
